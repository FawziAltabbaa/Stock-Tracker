📚 How to Read the Dashboard
Understand each metric in simple terms
×
▶ 📊 SCORE - Overall Ranking

This is your overall "buy signal" score combining all factors.
60+ = Excellent pick 🟢
50-59 = Good opportunity 🔵
40-49 = Worth watching 🟠
<40 = Proceed with caution 🔴

▶ 📈 MOMENTUM - Price Trend

Is the stock going up or down? Higher score = stronger uptrend.
Think of it like a car's speed - fast upward = positive momentum.
Tip: High momentum often means recent good news or earnings.

▶ 💰 VALUATION - Is It Cheap?

Is the stock price reasonable for what you're buying?
Higher score = better value for your money (like a sale).
Tip: Look for high valuations + high momentum = "growth at reasonable price"

▶ 💬 SENTIMENT - News Vibes

What are people saying about this stock in the news?
Higher score = more positive headlines and buzz.
Tip: Positive news might already be "priced in" - don't chase momentum alone.

▶ 🏷️ P/E RATIO - Price Per Dollar of Earnings

How much you're paying for each $1 of the company's earnings.
Lower is better! P/E of 15 is cheaper than P/E of 30.
Tip: Compare with industry average. Tech stocks usually have higher P/E than banks.
