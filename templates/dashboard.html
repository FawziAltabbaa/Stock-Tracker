📖 How to Read the Dashboard

Ticker

Stock's shorthand code (AAPL = Apple, MSFT = Microsoft)

Score (0-100)

Overall ranking combining all factors. 60+ = Excellent, 50-59 = Good, 40-49 = Moderate, Below 40 = Poor

Momentum

Is the price going up? Looks at moving averages & recent trends. Higher = uptrend 📈

Valuation

Is it cheap or expensive? Based on P/E ratio. Higher = better value 💰

Sentiment

Are news headlines positive? AI analyzes recent articles. Higher = good news 😊

P/E Ratio

Price-to-Earnings ratio. How much you pay per $1 of company earnings. Lower = better value
